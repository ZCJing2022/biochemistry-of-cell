# Part II Practice Exam Audit

## Locked exam format

All three exams follow the same structure as the existing `data-analysis-learning-teaching` practice-midterm series:

- Recommended time: **120 minutes**
- Section A: **38 mixed-order multiple-choice questions**
- Section B: **5 integrated short-answer problems**
- Answers hidden initially in collapsible Quarto callouts
- Coverage table at the end
- Weeks 8–12 deliberately mixed rather than presented chronologically

## Distinctness audit

Exact MCQ-stem overlap:

- Exam 1 vs Exam 2: **0 / 38**

The three exams are therefore not simple reorderings of the same question set.

## Intended differences

- **Exam 1:** broad balanced simulation across all five weeks.
- **Exam 2:** more application and prediction questions.
- **Exam 3:** more synthesis across metabolism, oxidative phosphorylation, pharmacology, and signaling.

## Source boundaries

Questions are grounded in the Week 8–12 study guides and practice materials already constructed from:

- weekly MBB201 lecture presentations;
- Alberts et al. (2023), *Essential Cell Biology*, Sixth Edition;
- weekly learning objectives;
- supplied chapter quizzes;
- shared tutorial materials.

No outside content was added to expand the examinable scope.
