Compare each tissue by its emphasized role rather than treating the color names as definitions. White adipose tissue primarily stores triacylglycerol and has endocrine functions. Brown adipose tissue specializes in thermogenesis and contains mitochondria with UCP1. Beige adipocytes are distinguished by inducible thermogenic capacity in the course comparison.
