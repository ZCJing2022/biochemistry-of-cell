A useful way to construct the requested four- or five-sentence answer is to separate an energy diagram from a rate curve. On the energy diagram, **ΔG** compares product and reactant levels, whereas **activation energy** describes the barrier leading to the transition state. They address favorability and access to the reaction route, respectively. Lowering the barrier can increase speed without changing the endpoint difference.

On the rate curve, **Vmax** is the limiting velocity for the enzyme amount under the stated conditions. **Km** is the substrate concentration at half that velocity. Their units provide an immediate check: Vmax has rate units, such as μmol/min, while Km has concentration units, such as mM. Do not describe Km as half of Vmax; it is the concentration corresponding to half of Vmax. Keeping these two diagrams and their axes separate allows the final answer to define all four terms without treating a kinetic measurement as a thermodynamic quantity.
