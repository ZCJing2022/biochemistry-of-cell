# Week 8 Visual Pilot Audit

## Goal

Make the Week 8 study guide substantially more visual without changing the lecture-defined examinable scope.

## Visual modules added

1. **Week 8 conceptual roadmap** — metabolism → free energy → coupling → redox → enzymes → kinetics.
2. **ΔG decision map** — exergonic, equilibrium, endergonic.
3. **Pathway-driving map** — ATP coupling and product removal.
4. **Redox / NAD+–NADH map** — electron transfer from fuel through NADH.
5. **Activation-energy diagram** — catalyzed versus uncatalyzed pathways with the same overall ΔG.
6. **Michaelis–Menten saturation curve** — Vmax, half-Vmax, and Km.

## Source integration

- Week 8 lecture slides, especially 8:7–8:10, 8:14–8:17, 8:22–8:28;

## Public-site copyright rule

The public site does not embed textbook pages or textbook figures verbatim. Textbook concepts are redrawn into original course-specific schematics. This keeps the study guide visually rich while avoiding redistribution of copyrighted textbook artwork.
