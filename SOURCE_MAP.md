## Editorial rules

- Do not let textbook material expand the weekly guide beyond topics actually taught in the lecture.
- Use Alberts for deeper explanation, mechanisms, definitions, and conceptual connections.
- Use quiz files to reproduce the course's question style without copying whole banks verbatim.
- Use every listed weekly learning objective to generate at least one constructed-response question.
- Keep answers collapsed by default using Quarto callouts.
